What we need from you

Time

Typically 4–8 hours across Phase 1 from each key person involved in the scoped workflows.

That may include the owner or operations lead, a PM who runs the work daily, the estimator when pricing is relevant, and the person handling the administrative work.

That last perspective often reveals where the time is really going.

Documents

Depending on what’s in scope, we may ask for:

  • Contract templates and subcontract forms.
  • Scopes from recent jobs—one that went well and one that didn’t.
  • Your pricing structure, markups, and rate card.
  • Whatever procedures or checklists exist, however rough.
  • A real change order, RFI, pay app, or closeout package.
  • Any spreadsheet somebody maintains by hand.
Access
  • Read access to your project management system if you have one.
  • A sample project folder as it actually looks.
  • Permission to sit in on one recurring meeting.
A point of contact

One point of contact who can keep documents, access, and decisions moving.

Purpose-built systems

Not every construction problem fits an existing Construction OS capability. When the business case is real, we design and build a system around a specific workflow, process, or operating need.

The goal is not custom software for its own sake. We build where the problem is worth solving, and where the work can become part of a stronger reusable system over time.
