How Construction OS works

Put more of how your company works into a system your team can use.

  1. 01
    Construction Intelligence

    Know what to do.

    Construction knowledge grounded in real operating practice, configured around your company’s procedures, pricing, standards, contracts, and experience.

  2. 02
    Document Workbench

    Do the work.

    Turn project information and decisions into the scopes, PCOs, RFIs, agreements, reports, and other documents the job requires.

  3. 03
    Project IntelligenceIn development

    Know what needs attention.

    Surface the open decisions, aging changes, missing approvals, unresolved work, and exceptions that need action.

  4. 04
    Operations AutomationExtension layer

    Keep the routine work moving.

    Take repetitive, high-volume operational work off the team while keeping review and judgment with your people.
