Six places it shows up
01
Paperwork

Your PMs spend their best hours producing paperwork.

Change orders, RFIs, meeting minutes, reports, submittals, billing documentation—rebuilt from scratch, job after job.

02
Knowledge

Your best people are the only ones who know how the company works.

The senior PM everyone calls. The COO who answers every operational question. The estimator whose pricing judgment nobody else has.

03
Visibility

You’re chasing people to figure out what needs attention.

Open changes, aging RFIs, missing approvals, unsigned contracts, schedule risk—assembled by hand, one conversation at a time.

04
Operations

Too much routine operational work is still manual.

Invoices and receipts entered by hand. Safety documentation chased. Billing packages assembled the same way every month.

05
Back office

The paper never stops coming.

Invoices and receipts arriving by email, photo, PDF, and paper. Compliance documents chased vendor by vendor. Cost data going stale because the paperwork is behind the work.

06
Margin

You do work you never get paid for.

The owner asks for something in the field, it gets done, and it never becomes a change order. A sub’s exclusion nobody caught before award. A scope item that disappeared during buyout.
