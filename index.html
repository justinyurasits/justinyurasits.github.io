We don’t start from scratch.

Construction OS already contains the architecture for recurring construction work—the documents, the scopes, the pricing conventions, the change procedures, the operating knowledge. We configure it around your company’s documents, rules, terminology, and judgment.

What Construction OS is not

Not project management

Procore and Buildertrend run the project. We produce the paperwork the project generates. Most firms run both.

Not an accounting system

We produce the pay application. Your books stay where they are.

Not a template library

A template doesn’t know the contract value.

Not a legal service

Every document is a draft for your review. You issue it, under your name.
