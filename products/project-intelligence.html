Where it fits

See the exception. Know what to do. Produce what comes next.

Project Intelligence is designed to become the attention layer of Construction OS.
It surfaces the exception. Construction Intelligence helps determine what should happen. Document Workbench helps produce the documentation needed to move the work forward.

Development approach

Built when the operation calls for it.

Project Intelligence is not being built just to fill out a product roadmap.

It will be developed where real client engagements show a need for better visibility across active projects—when important issues are aging quietly, leadership is spending too much time collecting status, or existing systems make it difficult to see where intervention is actually needed.
