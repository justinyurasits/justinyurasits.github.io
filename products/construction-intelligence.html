The knowledge problem

What happens when the person who knows leaves.

Your most experienced PMs carry years of decisions in their heads — how your company prices work, handles difficult owners, interprets its contracts, and applies its standards. When they’re in a meeting, on another job, or gone, that knowledge isn’t available.

Procedures exist. They’re not read.

The knowledge is written down somewhere. It lives in procedure manuals, past project files, and email threads. It’s not findable when it’s needed.

New PMs learn by asking.

The fastest way to get a company-specific answer is to find the right person. That’s expensive and inconsistent, and it stops working when the person isn’t available.

Knowledge walks out the door.

When experienced people leave, years of company-specific judgment goes with them. The procedures stay. The nuance doesn’t.

Construction Intelligence captures it.

Your procedures, standards, and project history become a knowledge base your whole team can query. The answer your best PM would give is the answer the system gives.
